Anti Desk Hogging

Restrict users from booking the same desk on repeat, to encourage cross-team interaction.

Floorsense has a feature to restrict users from booking the same desk multiple days in a row. Some customers enable this to encourage cross-team interaction and to prevent a return to a quasi-fixed-desk way of working. It works by setting a time window — e.g. 2 days — and how many of those days the same desk may be booked.
