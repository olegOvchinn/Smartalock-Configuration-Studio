Anti Desk Hogging

Restrict users from booking the same desk on repeat, to encourage cross-team interaction.

Floorsense has a feature to restrict users from booking the same desk multiple days in a row. Some customers enable this to encourage cross-team interaction and to prevent a return to a quasi-fixed-desk way of working. It works by setting a rolling time window, and separately, how many times within any window the same desk may be booked.

times within the rolling window (max 5)
