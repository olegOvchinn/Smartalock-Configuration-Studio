Desk numbering

Number your desks

Upload your floor plans, tap each desk to number it, label the rooms, and save the numbered plans together with a desk list.

Good to know

Desk number placement does not need to be perfect. Floorsense will not use this image in the app; it is only kept as our reference, so tapping close to each desk is enough.

This page and the Neighbourhood Mapper are used one after the other: desk numbering first, then the mapper. Desk numbering needs to be completed. The mapper is optional, unless you have chosen to use desk groups and user groups, in which case it is required too.

Upload your floor plans

Drag one or more images here, or choose files. Clean JPG or PNG images of about 1–2 MB each work best.

Room naming

Next room:

With Label rooms selected, each click names the next room automatically. Click a room label to type a real name such as “Boardroom”.

Click a desk number or room label to edit its text, move it or delete it.
