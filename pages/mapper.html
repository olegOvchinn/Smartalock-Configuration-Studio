Before you upload: image requirements

Around 1–2 MB

A JPG or PNG of roughly 1–2 MB. Much larger files are slow to work with; much smaller ones are usually too blurry.

Sharp and clear

Walls, doors and room outlines should stay crisp when you zoom in. Aim for about 2000–4000 px on the long side. Use the original export, not a photo or a screenshot of a printout.

No desk numbers, no extra annotations

Remove desk numbers, furniture codes, dimensions, markups, title blocks and legends before exporting. The cleaner the plan, the clearer the coloured neighbourhoods will read.

One whole floor, upright

One floor per image, cropped to the floor area, not rotated or skewed. Upload one image per level and repeat this page for each.
