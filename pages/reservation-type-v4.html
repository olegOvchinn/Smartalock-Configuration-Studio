How are lockers reserved?

Select up to three models for this site. Combine them if different locker banks need different rules.

Admin Allocated — Fixed only

Selected

Permanently assign lockers to individual users. Lockers remain allocated until released by an administrator. Ideal for staff with dedicated lockers.

User Self-Allocated

Selected

Users choose their own locker at the kiosk or app. The reservation never expires automatically and stays allocated until the user or an administrator releases it.

Parcel Delivery

Selected

Lockers act as a drop-off and pickup point for courier deliveries and internal mail. Temporary access is granted to a courier or recipient rather than a standing personal reservation.
