No Swipe No Power (NSNP)

With NSNP on, a desk stays powered off until someone checks in with a valid badge swipe, then powered off again once their reservation ends — you can turn on a short grace period below to keep it on a little longer after that. Permanently assigned desks stay powered on all the time and aren't affected by NSNP. This is entirely optional — choose how you'd like to roll it out, or skip it if you won't be using it.

Without the relay units in place, NSNP won't work. Tick this once they're installed to choose how you'd like to roll it out below — until then, NSNP stays off.

Power grace period

By default, power turns off the moment a reservation ends. Turn this on to keep power on for a few extra minutes after that — handy for a desktop that needs time to save and shut down properly, unlike a laptop running on its own battery.
