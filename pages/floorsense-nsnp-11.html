No Swipe No Power (NSNP)

NSNP requires NSNP relay units installed at each desk to function — if a desk is occupied but nobody has swiped a valid badge, power to its outlets is cut until a valid swipe is detected. This is entirely optional. Choose how you'd like to roll it out, or skip it if you won't be using it.

Power grace period: 1h

0 min
Badge swipe
1h
Power cut

If no valid badge swipe is detected within 1h of a desk becoming occupied, power to its outlets is cut.
