Auto-Release on Idle

Free up desks that were reserved but never actually used.

If a desk sits unclaimed or unused for long enough, it's automatically released back to general availability. Use the drum below to set how long a desk may sit idle before that happens.

Auto-release after: 3h

0h

3h

If a desk sits unclaimed or unused for 3 hours, it's automatically released back to general availability — this can happen at any time of day, on any day.

This can happen at any time of day, independent of the reservation window on the previous step — it just keeps desks from sitting reserved but empty.
