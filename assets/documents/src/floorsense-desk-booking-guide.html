Floorsense Desk Booking Guide

Everything you need to know about booking a desk

A quick, plain-English guide to how check-in, booking and privacy work — no jargon, just the essentials.

Booking & Check-in

Check in any time within your window

You can check in to your desk starting 1 hour before your booking begins, up to 1 hour after it starts. If you don't check in within that window, your desk becomes available to others.

Example: 8:00 AM booking  →  check in between 7:00–9:00 AM

Plan up to 8 days ahead

Need to plan your week? You can book a desk as much as 8 days before you need it.

Your desk is yours for 9 hours

Every booking lasts 9 hours by default — enough for a full working day, without needing to think about it.

One desk at a time, by default

You can have one desk reserved at once. Some teams or workplaces raise this limit — if you're not sure how many desks you're able to book, check with your workplace team.

Tapping In & Out

Tap once to arrive, tap again to leave

Your first tap of the day checks you in. When you're done, tap again at the same desk to check out. That's it.

Sitting somewhere else? No problem

If you booked one desk but end up checking in at a different one, we'll automatically release your original booking — so nobody else is left waiting on a desk you're not using.

Ways to Check In

Use whatever's easiest for you

Check in with your building access card, the Floorsense mobile app, the desktop app, or a wallet pass on your phone. Wallet pass may not be available on every plan — ask your workplace team if you're not sure.

Your Privacy

Need quiet time? Turn on Do Not Disturb

Set your status to Do Not Disturb whenever you need heads-down focus time — it lets colleagues know not to interrupt you.

Not switched on yet at your workplace

  • Sit-stand desk controls
  • Recurring (repeating) bookings
  • Team bookings, booking on behalf of a colleague, and custom notifications

If your workplace turns any of these on, they'll arrange training so you know how it works.

This guide covers Floorsense's standard, best-practice setup. A few details can vary by workplace — if something here doesn't match what you see in the app, check with your workplace or IT team.
